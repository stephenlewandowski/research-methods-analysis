# Research Methods Analysis

Code, datasets, and projects for teaching RSCH650 research methods.

- [Code](code/): analysis scripts, demonstrations, and simulations.
- [Datasets](datasets/): teaching data with source, citation, and variable documentation.
- [Projects](projects/): exercises and projects that connect research questions with analysis.

## Simulations

- [Two-sided Welch t-test](code/welch-t-test/): explore sample size, variability, p-values, confidence intervals, false positives, and power with two independent groups.

## Teaching packages

- [The t-test on the airport ramp](projects/t-test-teaching-package/): a 2:06 captioned animation, offline player, instructor notes, synthetic dataset, and SPSS syntax, with an independent quality review.

Each contribution should include enough context to understand its purpose and reproduce its results. Document dependencies, setup, inputs, and random seeds where applicable. Cite data sources and confirm permission to redistribute data before adding it.
